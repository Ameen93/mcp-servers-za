FAQ

Common buyer questions

What is MCP?

Model Context Protocol is a standard that lets AI systems call external tools safely and predictably.

Do I need Stitch specifically?

No. We’re Stitch-first for speed, but the architecture supports additional SA payment providers.

Can we self-host?

Yes. OSS core is self-hosted. Pro/Enterprise adds managed reliability and support.

Is this production-ready?

The MCP contract layer is in place now; live provider integrations are being wired and hardened.
